It's a comforting thought. But clients with other options absolutely remember.

The more interesting question is how you arrived at a crisis at 5PM on a Friday. There was almost always a Monday when someone saw it coming and decided that raising it carried more professional risk than staying quiet. The relationship lesson isn't about crisis management style — it's about whether you've built a team where the bad news gets surfaced on Monday, not discovered on Friday.

Handle the crisis well, by all means. But the Friday crisis is a symptom. The people lesson runs earlier than you think.
