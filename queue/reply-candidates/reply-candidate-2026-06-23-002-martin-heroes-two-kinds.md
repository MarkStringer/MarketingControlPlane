Mostly right, with one split worth making. There are two kinds of hero and only one of them is a bug.

The first quietly absorbs the breakage, works the weekend, and keeps the dashboard green. That one really is an organizational failure in disguise, because the heroics get spent hiding the data instead of surfacing it.

The second is the person who finally says the thing nobody wants to hear and then acts on it. That is not a system failure. That is usually the only thing that gets a stuck project moving, and no blameless retro will manufacture it on schedule.

All projects are swamps. You can engineer out the first hero. You cannot 5-whys your way to a project that never needs the second, because some of the uncertainty is real and someone still has to commit. Bad news is data. The hero worth keeping is the one who delivers it early.
