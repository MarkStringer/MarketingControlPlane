The burnout isn't primarily from absorbing stress. It's from being accountable for outcomes you don't control.

PMs don't just carry the organisational anxiety — they're positioned at exactly the point where the plan meets reality and told to manage that gap. Often with less authority than the plan assumed they'd have, and with information that arrives later and more filtered than it needs to.

"Protect your energy" is true and useful. But it treats the symptom. The underlying condition is structural: an expectation of ownership without the authority to match it. Self-care can help you survive that. It doesn't change what's being asked.

The organisations that want sustainable PMs need to reckon honestly with what they're asking one person to carry — and whether the accountability they're handing over is matched by the authority that person actually has. Usually it isn't.
