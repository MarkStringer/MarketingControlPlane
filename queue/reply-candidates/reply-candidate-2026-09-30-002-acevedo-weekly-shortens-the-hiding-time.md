I'd agree with the weekly rhythm and quarrel with the reason.

Weekly works because it shortens how long a problem can sit unseen. A quarterly review gives a slipping piece of work three months to stay quiet. A weekly one gives it six days. That's the whole mechanism, and it needs no talent or funding.

Where I'd be careful is the idea that weekly exposes who is delivering. If people learn the meeting exists to find the ones who aren't, they'll bring you a tidier version of the week. You get the cadence and lose the information it was meant to carry.

Bad news is data. The weekly meeting is worth having only if the first person to say "this is behind" is treated as someone who saved you a month.

Short, frequent, and safe to be honest in. Drop the third and the first two just make the theatre run more often.
