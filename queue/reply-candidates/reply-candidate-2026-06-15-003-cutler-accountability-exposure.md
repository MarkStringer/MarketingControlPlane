Accountability without authority isn't accountability. It's exposure.

Most PMs I know are technically accountable for project delivery. What they're rarely given is the power to change the things that determine whether delivery is possible. They can't move the deadline. They can't change the resourcing. They can't stop scope changes arriving from three directions above them.

They can report. They can escalate. They can wave their hands. And when the project fails, they're accountable.

Vague ownership burns people out — and so does precise ownership of things you don't control. Naming someone as accountable for an outcome they can't influence isn't an organisational design. It's a blame structure.
