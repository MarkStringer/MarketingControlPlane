You have named the symptom. The cause underneath it is that a framework is a machine for not having a point of view.

A scoring matrix takes a judgment call, the kind someone could be blamed for, and launders it into a number. Now nobody has to be the person who said this and not that. The framework decided. The framework cannot be fired.

Point of view is worth 80 IQ points. The teams that prioritise well are not the ones with the better matrix. They are the ones with someone willing to say where they are placing the bet and be wrong about it in public.

A North Star helps, but only because it gives that person something to be wrong against. The framework was never the problem. The missing point of view was.
