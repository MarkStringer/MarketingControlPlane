This is what a merger actually is underneath the press release: two organisations that have spent decades solving different problems with different rulebooks, now expected to run on one calendar.

Paper mills think in eighteen-month capacity bets. Pharma thinks in FDA batch cycles and shelf life. Both sides have been swamps in their own right for years. Merging them doesn't drain either swamp, it just moves the waterline.

The $2.1B synergy number is the interesting part, not because it's wrong, but because of what happens to it next. Someone signed off on that figure before the integration team had found any of this. In eighteen months, when the number and the reality have drifted apart, the question worth asking isn't who missed the target. It's who's still around to own the gap between the bet that was made and the swamp that was actually there.
