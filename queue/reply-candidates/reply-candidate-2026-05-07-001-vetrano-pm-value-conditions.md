The shift from control to enabling is real and worth making.

But "creating conditions for value to flow" carries a hidden assumption: that the value has been honestly defined before you start creating conditions for it.

Most projects fail not in execution but before it. The value was described in terms that sounded good in the proposal, got approved, and was never seriously re-examined by the people who'd actually have to use the thing.

Host leadership works when the room is genuinely aligned on what good looks like. The harder work is getting the room to stop pretending it's aligned when it isn't.

Deliver the possible, not the fantasy. And the first work is usually finding out what the possible actually is.
