The last line is the one I would keep. Organisations behave according to what systems reward. Anybody who has spent a year on a project inside a large company has felt the truth of that in their teeth.

But I would argue with the word invisible, because I think it lets everyone off too lightly.

The current is not a force of nature. Every incentive, every metric, every approval forum, every rule about who can spend what was put there on purpose, by somebody, usually to fix something that had just gone wrong. Each one of them was somebody's version of a chief systems designer doing exactly what the post asks for. Making the right outcome the default. It worked, for the outcome they had in mind at the time.

So what dissolves the bold vision is not an absence of deliberate systems. It is a surplus of them. It is the last three visions, built, and still running.

Here's the thing about taking them apart. Systems are not hard to remove because you cannot see them. They are hard to remove because each one has an owner, and the owner is still in the building, and was promoted for building it. Retiring a metric is telling a person their work is finished. Closing an approval forum is telling six people they no longer matter on Tuesdays.

Which is why redesign so rarely means removal. It is far cheaper to add a new system on top of the old ones than to fight anybody over the old ones. The new incentive goes in. The old incentive stays. Now there are two, and they disagree, and the people on the ground quietly decide which one is real. That is how the current got strong. Not through neglect. Through a series of perfectly deliberate additions, none of which anyone was ever paid to subtract.

And there is a harder problem underneath. Systems that make the right outcome the default are, by construction, systems that reduce variance. A bold vision is the opposite. It is a bet. It only needs a designer because the existing systems were designed to stop exactly this kind of thing from happening.

So the systems this CEO builds to make her outcomes the default are the ones the next bold vision will dissolve in. The current she is swimming against was somebody's vision too. It just got built.
