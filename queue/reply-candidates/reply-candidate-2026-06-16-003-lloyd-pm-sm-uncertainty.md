Large deliverables versus incremental delivery is the wrong distinction.

The split between PM work and Scrum Master work isn't about the size of the deliverable. It's about the uncertainty. A large deliverable in familiar territory can be planned and tracked with a traditional PM approach. A small deliverable in genuinely uncertain territory needs iteration not because it's small, but because you don't know what you're building yet.

The problem with putting PMs on "large deliverables" and Scrum Masters on "incremental" is the assumption that you can tell in advance which kind of project you have. Most of the time, you can't. You find out once you're in it.

All projects are swamps. Some just have better maps.
