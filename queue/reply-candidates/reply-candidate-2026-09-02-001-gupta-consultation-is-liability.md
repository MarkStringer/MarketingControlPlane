The pairs are real. I have been on both ends of all of them.

But I do not think the variable is temperament.

Look at what each complaint is protecting. "Why are you wasting my time with half-baked ideas" is a refusal to be on the record before the idea is safe. "Why wasn't I consulted on this decision" is a complaint about being made a witness to a decision instead of a party to it.

Those are not two settings on a communication dial. That is one person managing one thing, which is how much of this they can be held to if it goes wrong.

The detail pairs work the same way. A senior exec asking for the bottom line up front is not asking for less information. They are asking for the version they can repeat upwards without having to defend the workings.

Which is why the cadence never settles. You are not calibrating preference. You are negotiating, one person at a time, who is standing next to you when the project turns out to be a bet that did not come off.

The exhausting part is not that there are twelve games. It is that nobody will say out loud which one they are playing.
