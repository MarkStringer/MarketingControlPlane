Sales wants speed. Finance wants cost cuts. Engineering wants stability. Those can't all be true.

You're right that this isn't a coordination problem. But it's not a PM problem either.

The PM is being asked to resolve, at the project level, a conflict the organisation hasn't resolved at the strategic level. If the executive team hasn't agreed on what the company is actually optimising for, the project plan won't fix that. The PM can make the contradiction visible — put it in writing, escalate it, get it onto a steering group agenda. Making it visible and getting it resolved are two different things.

The gap between those two is where most projects die. Most organisations then blame the PM.
