Agreed that late is bad. But "can you create the communications and training?" is a more useful request than it sounds.

To write the training you have to say what someone will do on Monday that they didn't do on Friday. To write the comms you have to say why they would. If the design can't answer either, you have found the problem, and you found it by doing exactly what you were asked.

The project is a bet that certain people will behave in a certain way. It was probably placed without anyone saying so. The training plan is often the first document that has to spell it out.

Mandate and protection would be lovely. While you're waiting, take the blank where the reason should be back to whoever approved the timeline and ask them to fill it in. It is very hard to call that disloyalty. They asked for the leaflet.
