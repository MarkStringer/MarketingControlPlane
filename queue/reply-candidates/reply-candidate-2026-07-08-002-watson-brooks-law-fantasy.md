Brooks's Law is right, but it usually gets quoted to answer the wrong question. Everyone treats it as "how do we make a late project faster", and the answer is you can't, more people makes it later.

The thing is, the project didn't become late when you noticed. It became late the day the plan pretended the work was smaller than it was. Adding engineers is what people reach for because it looks like action, and because it lets everyone avoid the actual conversation, which is that the scope was a fantasy and something now has to give.

Small coordinated teams are the fix for the next project. For this one the honest move is to change what you promised, not who's carrying it. Deliver the possible, not the fantasy.
