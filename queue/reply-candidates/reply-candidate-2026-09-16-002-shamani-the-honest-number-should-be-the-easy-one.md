This maps exactly onto why status reports lie. Nobody sits down each week and decides, with full
motivation, to hide the number. The environment just makes the honest version harder to file than
the tidy one, so the tidy one wins by default, over and over, the same way the sofa wins over the
gym.

The fix is not a braver team, and it is not even a franker one. It is redesigning the environment so
the true number is the path of least resistance. A one line field for "what changed since last week
and why" does more for a project's honesty than any exhortation to speak up. Bad news is data, but
only if the process makes handing it over the automatic behaviour rather than the effortful one.

All projects are swamps. The only question is whether the reporting environment makes wading in the
default, or makes pretending the water is shallow the easier motion.
