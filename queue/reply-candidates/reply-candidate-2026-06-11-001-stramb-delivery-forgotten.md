The sponsor who went to bat for this project absolutely remembers if it delivered.

The Friday 5PM crisis usually isn't bad luck. It's what happens when Wednesday's warning signs were smoothed into "challenges" before they reached decision-makers. The crisis that blows up at 5PM was wobbling at 11AM on Thursday. How you handle the moment is downstream of how you managed the information before it.

Caring about people and caring about delivery aren't opposites. The teams that hold together under pressure are usually the ones where bad news travelled fast enough to be useful. You don't get that by being a good human at 5PM on Friday. You get it by making it safe to say the hard thing on Tuesday morning.
