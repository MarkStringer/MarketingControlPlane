The 60/40 split feels right. What I'd push back on is the conclusion.

The 60% you're describing — stakeholder politics, navigating dependencies, managing upwards — isn't underperforming because it lacks process documentation. It's hard because it depends on context, power, and whether the people with authority in the organisation actually want what the project is supposed to deliver.

No template changes that.

The PM isn't burnt out from insufficient standards. They're burnt out because they're accountable for outcomes they don't control, with less authority than the project plan assumed they'd have.

Adding standardisation to that situation mostly adds more things to complete documentation for.

The organisations that fix this don't do it by improving the checklists. They do it by being honest about what they're asking one person to carry — and whether the accountability they're handing over is matched by the authority that person actually has.

Usually it isn't.
