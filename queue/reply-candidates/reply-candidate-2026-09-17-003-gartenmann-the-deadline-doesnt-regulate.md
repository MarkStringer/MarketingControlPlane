The physiology is probably right. I am not about to argue with a nervous system researcher about nervous systems.

But notice what the fix quietly becomes: capacity, presence, regulation. All properties of the individual leader, sitting there under pressure, trying to stay open instead of collapsing into control.

The project does not wait for anyone's nervous system to catch up. The deadline is still the deadline while a leader is finding their capacity to stay present. If the only channel bad news has to travel upward runs through one person's regulated state, you have not fixed the reporting problem. You have made it depend on how well that person slept.

Deliver the possible, not the fantasy, means giving bad news somewhere to go that does not require heroics from whoever happens to be holding it that day. Build that route and the nervous system stops being load bearing. Do not, and you are one bad night's sleep away from the numbers not moving until it is too late to do anything about them.
