The teams usually understand Scrum well enough. The problem is the environment they're implementing it in.

Honest sprint reviews require that someone can say publicly: this didn't work, and here's why. Retrospectives require that the people in the room believe saying uncomfortable things won't affect how they're treated afterward. Product owners need genuine authority to refuse work, not just the title. When those conditions don't exist, teams correctly adjust their behavior. They run Scrum on the surface in a way that looks like the framework while protecting themselves from what the framework actually requires.

That is not a failure to understand Scrum. It is a sensible response to the actual incentive structure.
