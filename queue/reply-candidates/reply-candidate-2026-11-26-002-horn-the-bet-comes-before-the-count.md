I think the last line is the important one, and the reason it's hard to count is partly timing.

Tokens, pull requests and points all report by Friday. Whether the user's life got better reports in a few months, if anyone goes to look, by which time the team is on something else and the budget conversation has happened.

So the proxy wins by turning up first.

What helps is treating the work as a bet and saying what the bet is before you start. Which user, doing what, differently, and when we're going to go and see. It can be crude. One person watched trying to do the thing is allowed.

You can lose a bet like that, which is what makes it useful. Nobody has ever lost a bet on consuming tokens.
