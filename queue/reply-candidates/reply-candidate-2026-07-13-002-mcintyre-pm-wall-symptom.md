Agree the wall is inefficient. I'd be careful about treating it as only a habit to correct, though, because it's usually a symptom of something real.

Ask why teams want protecting from their PM in the first place. In a lot of places it's because the PM's job has quietly become converting what the team knows into a story the people above want to hear. Red becomes amber, amber becomes green, "we're stuck" becomes "actively managing dependencies." When that's the arrangement, the team isn't being precious. It's protecting its own data from someone who has been rewarded for softening it.

Embed a PM like that and you haven't fixed anything. You've just moved the laundry closer to the source.

Embedding works, and I'm for it, but only when the PM is loyal to what's true rather than to the report. Sit inside the team, see the mess first hand, then carry it upward without sanding off the corners. That's the version worth having. Bad news is data, and the PM's real job is making sure it survives the trip up the hierarchy intact.
