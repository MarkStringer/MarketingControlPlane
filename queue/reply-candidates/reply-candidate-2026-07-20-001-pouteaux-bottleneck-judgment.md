You're right that AI didn't create the bottleneck. But I'd go one further. It didn't move it either. The constraint was always the same thing. It was just hiding behind the time it took to type.

Writing code was never the slow part of a hard project. The slow part was deciding what was worth building, and then having someone in the room competent enough to tell whether the thing that got built was any good. That work doesn't speed up when the typing does. It just gets more exposed.

A review queue full of AI-generated PRs nobody has the energy to read isn't a throughput problem. It's a judgment problem wearing a throughput costume. And judgment is the one thing you can't 10x by adding compute.

All projects are swamps. AI just drained the shallow end and let everyone see how deep the middle was all along.
