"I'll try to get this done" isn't a clarity problem. It's the most precise sentence in the whole exchange.

It tells you the person doesn't think they'll make it and hasn't found a safe way to say so. Swap it for "I'll deliver X by Friday" and you haven't fixed anything. You've just made the lie sound more confident.

Vague language is usually a survival strategy, not a vocabulary gap. People go fuzzy when being specific gets them punished. On a team where bad news is treated as data, people are already precise, because precision is cheap and safe. On a team where it isn't, your ten better phrases just produce ten crisper fictions.

Fix what happens to the person who says "this is going to be late" out loud. The clarity follows.
