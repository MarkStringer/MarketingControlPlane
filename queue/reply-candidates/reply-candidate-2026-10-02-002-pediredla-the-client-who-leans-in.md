The part about leaning in rather than blaming is the one I'd push on.

Nobody walks into a project as the kind of client who retreats into blame. It gets trained in the first time the team brings bad news. If that news is met with curiosity, the next piece arrives sooner. If it's met with a hunt for who's responsible, the next piece arrives late and polished.

All projects are swamps, so there will be a first piece of bad news. Bad news is data, and how it's received decides whether you get more of it.

So I'd add one test to your fit question. Ask a prospective client about the last time something went wrong, and listen to what they did in the first hour.
