You're right that the plan is the work, not the admin. But it's worth being clear about what a maintained plan actually buys you.

It maps the work you can already see. The go-live date isn't a number you compute off that map. It's a bet you place on it. Projects don't usually slip on the tasks that were in the plan. They slip on the ones that weren't there yet, the ones you only find once you've walked into that part of the swamp.

Which is why the line about stakeholders wanting confidence rather than updates is the dangerous one. A stakeholder who wants confidence more than they want data will get a date and never get the truth. Bad news is data. The plan's real job isn't to manufacture a number everyone can feel good about. It's to show you fast when the bet has changed.
