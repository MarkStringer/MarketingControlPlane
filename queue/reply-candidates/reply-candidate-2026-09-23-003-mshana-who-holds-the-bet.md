The mindset difference is real, but it isn't really about contractors versus employees. It's about who's holding the bet.

A contractor's invoice is tied to metres drilled and tonnes hauled. Miss the number and they feel it in the same month, in their own account. An owner team hits payroll either way.

That's the whole trick. Give someone a stake in the outcome and they behave like it's their money on the table, because eventually it is. Leave them salaried and insulated from the result, and the project stops being their bet and starts being their job.

You don't fix that with a clarity poster in the break room. You fix it by finding some way to make the outcome land on the person doing the work, the way it already lands on the contractor.
