I agree the idea is rarely the bottleneck. But "someone took ownership and made it happen" hides what actually went on in the room.

Ownership is two things people don't much like naming. The first is enough authority to actually move things, budget, people, a sponsor who will pick up the phone. The second is the willingness to commit before you are anywhere near certain. The person who "made it happen" almost always had cover to be wrong. Take that cover away and the most determined owner just becomes the one who gets blamed when the bet doesn't land.

Point of view is worth 80 IQ points, but only if the organisation lets you act on it. Plenty of sharp people have watched a good idea die while holding all the ownership and none of the authority.

So by all means hand someone the project. Just check you've handed them the room to be wrong in, because ownership without that isn't ownership, it's exposure with a nicer job title.
