"One small thing" is rarely small and it is never new. It was in the project all week. It just had no reason to reach the status meeting until someone said it out loud on Friday.

Green does not mean the project is fine. It means nobody has gone looking. It is not a measurement of the work, it is a measurement of how hard people are willing to poke it before the weekend. All projects are swamps. The water was up to your knees on Tuesday. The status board only ever shows the bit above the surface.

The fix is not a better dashboard. It is rewarding whoever brings the small thing in on Tuesday instead of punishing the person who turns the board red. Bad news is data. A project that goes green, green, green, red was not fine until Friday. It was a project where the data had nowhere to go.
