Agree with the split, but I'd move the line.

Most of your experience examples are attentiveness when things are going well. Weekly updates on a smooth job. Remembering the parking worry. All good, all easy. Anyone competent can be a delightful partner on a green project.

The relationship is made or broken on the day it goes wrong. The irreplaceable partner is the one who walks in and says "this is slipping and here's why" before the client works it out for themselves. That's the update nobody wants to send, and it's the only one that actually buys you trust, because it costs you something to send it.

Bad news is data, and being the person who hands it over early, while it's still cheap to act on, is most of the job. The parking follow-ups are the nice bit on top.
