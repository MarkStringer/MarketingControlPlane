post_summary: Klebine describes "project management chicken" — five PMs all reporting green, each waiting to see who admits slippage first, then rebasing. He proposes three tactics to surface truth earlier: private pre-board checkpoints, financial framing of delays, and publicly rewarding honesty. His central claim is "false green is not optimism — it is the exorbitant tax you pay for delayed truth."
---

The tactics are sensible but they treat the symptom. PMs play chicken because the last PM who called red early got blamed for the slippage. Until organisations stop penalising honest status, private checkpoints and financial framing just rearrange chairs.

Bad news is data. The question is whether the organisation is set up to receive it. When it isn't, no amount of clever meeting structure changes the calculation.
