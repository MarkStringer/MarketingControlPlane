Agreed that planning is not the timeline, and I like "planning is synonymous with project definition." I'd push on one word though. Planning is the most useful thing you can do before you start, and it still is not control.

The plan is a bet on a future you cannot see yet. A very good plan is a very well-researched bet. It is not a grip on events. All projects are swamps, and you can map the swamp before you walk into it, you absolutely should, but the map does not drain it.

The risk in calling planning "control" is that the better the plan looks, the more the organisation believes the swamp has gone away. That makes it harder, not easier, to report the first sign that it hasn't. So plan hard and plan continuously, exactly as you say. Just sell it upstairs as the best available bet rather than control, and keep the channel open for the bad news, because that is the data telling you the bet has moved.
