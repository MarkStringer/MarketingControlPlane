The title argument is the wrong argument. Lead, Manager, Coordinator, Assistant. None of those words tell you the only thing that matters, which is whether you're allowed to change anything.

I've held the Manager title on projects where I could move nothing, and no title at all on ones where a quiet word from me shifted the whole thing. The org chart measures permission badly. What you can actually influence is the real job description, and it rarely matches the card on the door.

So when someone says "I'm just a Project Lead," I don't rush to correct them. Sometimes it's underselling. But sometimes it's an accurate read of a role that carries the responsibility for delivery and none of the authority to affect it, which is the worst seat in the building.

Don't argue about the label. Find out what you're actually allowed to move, and whether that matches what you'll be blamed for. That gap is the whole game.
