Understanding the problem is the right instinct, but it is still a guess. The diagnosis that customers are leaving because of delivery is a bet, and most teams never write it down as one. They fund the fix and then read every result as support for it.

State the diagnosis as a claim you could lose, and decide before you spend what number would tell you it is wrong. Then bad news is data about the diagnosis, not an argument about the effort.
