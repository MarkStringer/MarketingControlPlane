Swap "patient" for "project team" and this is a failure I see constantly, just wearing different words.

A project falls behind. The organisation's answer is more process: another status template, another stakeholder review, another refresher on the tool nobody was trained to use properly. All of it assumes the team is the broken part.

Usually the team is fine. What's missing sits upstream of them: a budget that arrived late, a decision that never got made, a dependency that was never really available on the date the plan assumed. The team is standing in a swamp somebody else built, and getting blamed for being wet.

Your fix for healthcare applies here word for word. Design for the conditions people are actually working in, not the ones the org chart assumes. Everything else is asking the person closest to the failure to personally compensate for a system nobody upstream is willing to change.
