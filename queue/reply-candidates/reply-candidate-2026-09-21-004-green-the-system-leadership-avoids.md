That's the right diagnosis, and it generalises further than CRM adoption.

Every project has a Salesforce and a Slack. The formal system is the RAG report, the weekly status deck, the risk register. The Slack is the corridor conversation, the "just tell me straight" call the sponsor takes instead of reading the document someone built for exactly that purpose.

The moment a sponsor gets their real answer from the corridor conversation, the formal system stops being where the truth lives. It becomes theatre that runs in parallel with the actual decision-making channel, and everyone on the project can tell the difference even if nobody says so out loud.

Bad news is data, but only the channel leadership actually uses counts as a channel. You can build the most honest status reporting process in the world and it will still die the moment the CEO shows people that Slack gets a faster, franker answer. The system isn't unused because people are lazy. It's unused because leadership already told everyone, by their own behaviour, which one is real.
