Managing expectations gets filed under soft skills, which is how it ends up untrained. It isn't soft. It's the hardest negotiation on the project.

Here's the thing. Every stakeholder walks in with a private version of what done looks like, and most of those versions can't all be true at once. Managing expectations isn't listening warmly until everyone feels heard. It's working out whose version you're going to disappoint, and doing it early enough that the disappointment is still cheap.

The PMs who are bad at this usually aren't bad listeners. They're conflict-avoidant, so they let every expectation survive untouched until the deadline forces the reckoning at the worst possible moment.

Intuition and listening are how you find out what people expect. Telling them what they're actually going to get is the job. Deliver the possible, not the fantasy.
