Five times faster than the rest of the team. The interesting question is not about adoption.

When one person gets 5x faster with AI and nobody else can replicate it, the instinct is to diagnose a documentation problem. But what it usually reveals is that their judgment was doing most of the work all along. The AI is executing their model of the project — the tolerances, the client preferences, the things that never made it into any brief.

You can document the steps. You cannot document when to apply them and which details actually matter.

"Treat it like a process change" addresses the symptom. The real question is why the project's shared understanding is concentrated in one person. That is a project structure problem, and no amount of workflow documentation solves it.
