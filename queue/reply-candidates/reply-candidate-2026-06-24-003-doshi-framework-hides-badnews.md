Agreed, and I would go one step further. No product fails because of the framework. But the wrong framework can stop you noticing that it is failing.

A process that turns every standup green, that converts a stuck team into a tidy burndown, is not neutral. It is suppressing information. The people doing the work can see the trouble, and the system has nowhere for them to put it.

Bad news is data. The one thing a method has to get right is making it cheap and safe to surface bad news early. Everything else is decoration. A framework that hides the swamp is worse than no framework at all.
