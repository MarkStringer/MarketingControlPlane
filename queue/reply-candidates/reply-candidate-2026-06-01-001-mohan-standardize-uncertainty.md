The 40/60 split rings true. But the 60% isn't improvised because no one got around to systematising it.

It's improvised because the information wasn't there yet.

You can't write a standard operating procedure for "navigate the thing we didn't know we didn't know." The firefighting isn't a gap in your process library. It's the project telling you something it was hiding at the start.

All projects are swamps. The 30% firefighting is the terrain, not a failure to prepare properly. High-performing PMs don't eliminate the fires — they learn from them faster. That's a different skill from standardisation, and it doesn't come from a template.
