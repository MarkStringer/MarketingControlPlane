True but incomplete. Saying no to an unrealistic timeline is the easy part once you have the credibility to say it. The harder job is figuring out what's actually possible and making that case. If the only thing you're offering is a no, without an alternative, you're generating discomfort without moving the project.

The PM who earns respect doesn't just push back. They deliver the possible, not the fantasy.
