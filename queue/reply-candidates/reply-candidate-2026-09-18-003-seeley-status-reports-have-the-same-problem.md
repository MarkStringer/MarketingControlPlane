Project status reports have the exact same problem. Percentage complete, tasks closed, a healthy-looking burn-down, all activity metrics, and all just as fakeable as a completion rate.

They survive not because anyone is being dishonest, but because activity metrics are always available on demand and outcome metrics are not. You can report "92% of tasks closed" on any Friday you like. You can only report that the thing actually works for the people who need it once it is true, and that might land later than the report is due.

So teams report what is available rather than what is true, and everyone quietly agrees not to notice the difference, because the alternative is admitting the outcome is not there yet.

Your question is the right one to force at the end of every status meeting, not just every course: "but what changed?" Ask it enough times and nobody can hide behind a green status any more, which is exactly why almost nobody asks it.
