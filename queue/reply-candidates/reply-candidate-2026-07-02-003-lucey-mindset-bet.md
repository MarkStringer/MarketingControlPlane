I don't think discipline and boldness are in tension. They're the same move seen from two sides.

The project is a bet. Once you accept that, the entrepreneur and the project manager stop being two mindsets you have to balance. The entrepreneur decides the bet is worth making. The project manager works out how much you're actually staking and what the real odds are. You need both, because a bet you can't size is just a hope with a deadline.

The advice not to let a few risks stop you is right, but it quietly assumes the risks are small. Sometimes they aren't, and the disciplined half of you is the part that can tell the difference. Boldness that won't look at the downside isn't courage. It's deciding to deliver the fantasy instead of the possible.
