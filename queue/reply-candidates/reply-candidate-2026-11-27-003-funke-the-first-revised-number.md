Showing plant managers the competitor numbers is the part I'd copy. It's hard to make a sensible offer about a position you haven't been allowed to see.

I'd be more careful with the commitments that came back above the ask. They may be exactly what you say they are. But real ownership and not wanting to look like the weak site can produce the same figure, especially just after a comparison.

So I'd treat each one as a bet, and have each site say what has to be true for it to come in and what should be visible a few months from now if it's working.

You're right that the value is in what happens afterwards. I'd look for it in the first plant manager who comes back to say their number was too high. How that conversation goes will tell you how two-way the first one was. Bad news is data, and a commitment nobody can safely revise stops producing any.
