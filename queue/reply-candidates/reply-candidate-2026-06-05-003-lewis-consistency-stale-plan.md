Consistency creates results. The question is: consistent with what?

Every project has at least two plans: the one in the document, and the one in the team's heads. As work progresses and uncertainty resolves, these diverge. The results you get come from the plan the team is actually following, which may no longer be the one anyone formally agreed to.

Governance rhythm and escalation discipline help. But only if they are oriented towards "is our current shared model of this project still accurate?" rather than "are we executing consistently with the original?"

Consistent execution of a stale plan is how you deliver the wrong thing on time.
