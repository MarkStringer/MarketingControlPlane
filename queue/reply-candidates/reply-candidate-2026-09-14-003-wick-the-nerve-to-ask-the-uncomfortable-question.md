The questions are right. The reason they get skipped isn't that nobody thought of them.

Ask "what would failure look like" in week one and you're not gathering requirements anymore, you're the person who brought failure into a room that's trying to feel confident. That costs something. Not much, but enough that most people quietly let the question go unasked rather than spend the credibility.

So the gap isn't a checklist gap. Strong BAs don't just know which questions to ask, they've worked out how to ask them without getting filed under "not a team player." That's harder than remembering the list, and it's the one thing that actually decides whether kickoff week does what you're describing.

Point of view is worth 80 IQ points, and in kickoff week that's mostly what it buys: the nerve to ask what everyone else is politely avoiding.
