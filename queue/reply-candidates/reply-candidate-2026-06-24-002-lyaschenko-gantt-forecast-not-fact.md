The chart is not the problem. The belief is.

A Gantt chart is a forecast drawn so neatly that people start treating it as a record of something that already happened. Every bar is a guess wearing a hard edge. The tidy lines hide the fact that all projects are swamps and the ground keeps moving under every dependency you drew.

Keep the chart if it helps you think. Just stop handing it to sponsors as a promise. Show them where the uncertainty actually lives instead. The honest version of a plan has the soft bits marked, not painted over.
