29% is a number people will read as a discipline problem. It isn't. It's a routing problem.

Employees don't sabotage strategies they think are already failing on their own. They sabotage ones they've watched leadership insist are working while the evidence in front of them says otherwise.

Bad news is data. If an organisation doesn't have a channel that will carry "this isn't working the way you're describing it" without punishing whoever says it, the message doesn't disappear. It goes underground and comes back out as low-quality output and unapproved workarounds instead.

Call it sabotage if you want the easy word for it. It's closer to the most honest feedback the AI strategy is going to get, arriving the only way the organisation left open.
