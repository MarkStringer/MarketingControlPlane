You put the real thing in a subordinate clause and then walked past it. "Given the managerial power dynamics." That is the whole post, and everything either side of it is the polite version.

The technical team does not get overshadowed because the delivery manager fails to understand the platform. It gets overshadowed because the delivery manager owns the date, and the date is the thing the organisation is actually measuring.

Which is why I would go further than you and say that more technical knowledge can make this worse, not better.

A delivery manager who fully understands the system is rare. A delivery manager who half understands it is everywhere, and that is the dangerous configuration, because half understanding is enough to argue. It lets you say surely we can just, fluently enough that refusing costs the engineer something. Now they are choosing between a twenty minute explanation to someone who outranks them and a quiet yes. They take the quiet yes. The estimate that comes out the other side is a fantasy with two signatures on it instead of one.

I have been that manager. I once talked a team down from three weeks to ten days using vocabulary I had picked up in a meeting the previous Thursday. It took five weeks. The cost of that was not the two extra weeks, it was that for the next year those engineers gave me numbers they thought I would accept rather than numbers they believed.

The genuinely valuable thing a non-technical delivery manager brings is the outsider's question. Why does that take three weeks. What breaks if we skip it. What are you actually worried about. Point of view is worth 80 IQ points, and the naive point of view is worth more than most, right up to the moment the person asking is also the person who writes your objectives. Then it stops being a question and becomes a request.

So I would not fix this with training. I would fix it by noticing that you have asked one person to do two incompatible jobs. Somebody has to hold the date. Somebody has to be safe to tell the truth to. When those are the same person you have not got collaboration, you have got a negotiation where one party already knows which answer they are going to accept.
