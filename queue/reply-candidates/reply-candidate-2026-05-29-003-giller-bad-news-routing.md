Knowing what each audience needs is half the diagnostic. The other half is harder.

Sometimes executives, sponsors, and teams don't just need different information. They need different versions of what's happening. The exec needs confidence. The sponsor needs no surprises. The team knows it's on fire.

The communication problem isn't usually that the PM doesn't know this. It's that delivering bad news to the right person at the right time carries a professional cost high enough that most people learn to route around it.

Point of view is worth 80 IQ points. But someone has to be willing to have the unpopular one.
