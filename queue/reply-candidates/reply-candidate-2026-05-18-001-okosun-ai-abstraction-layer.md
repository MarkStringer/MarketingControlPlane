There's an assumption buried here worth surfacing.

The argument is that AI handles routine coordination, freeing PMs for strategy. That's attractive. But it assumes coordination was the hard part.

The hard part of project management is epistemic: forming an honest picture of what's actually happening. A PM who faithfully reports bad news is doing something genuinely difficult. If AI generates the status report, it will be readable and coherent. What it won't necessarily do is ensure bad news makes it through.

Bad news is data. Getting it from the delivery team to the decision-maker, without it being softened or deprioritised along the way, is the real work. Adding an AI abstraction layer doesn't free you for strategy. It adds another filter between you and reality.
