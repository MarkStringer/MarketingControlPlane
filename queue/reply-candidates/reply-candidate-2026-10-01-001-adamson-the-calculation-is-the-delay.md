The part I'd add is that the calculation has a cost you can measure.

Every time someone runs a true thing through a filter first, it arrives later. Softened, then saved, then said once it can't be ignored. On a project that delay is the whole problem. A risk that could have been a conversation in week three is a recovery plan in week twelve.

Bad news is data. The trouble is that data held back to protect someone's feelings is data you don't get to act on.

So I'd test it by counting days. How long between someone knowing and the room knowing? That number tells you how safe the place is, and nobody has to run a workshop to find it.
