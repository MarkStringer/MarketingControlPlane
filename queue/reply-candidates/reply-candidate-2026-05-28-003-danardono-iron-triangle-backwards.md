The iron triangle is correct and also backwards.

By the time someone in a real overrun asks "what are our options?", the answer has usually already been decided. Which constraint gives way isn't a product of analysis — it's a product of who has the most protection in the organization. The sponsor who can't be told no. The scope that's politically untouchable. The deadline that was announced publicly before the project started.

The triangle gets drawn to ratify a decision, not to make one.
