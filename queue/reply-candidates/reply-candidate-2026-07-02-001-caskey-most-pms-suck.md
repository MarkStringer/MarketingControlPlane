The ones you're describing aren't bad at the job. They're good at a different job, one that happens to be worthless.

Reporting status, guarding documents, filling the meeting with their own voice. That's a real skill set. It just doesn't move a project.

The rare ones you rate are doing something else entirely. They treat bad news as data instead of a personal threat, and they get people to hand it over early. Everything else you listed, the technical learning, the accountability, the flexibility, follows from that one habit. If bad news is safe to report near you, you find out what's actually happening. Point of view is worth 80 IQ points, and theirs is built from information nobody else was willing to say out loud.

So most project managers don't suck. Most project manager roles are defined as the worthless version, and most people fill the role as written.
