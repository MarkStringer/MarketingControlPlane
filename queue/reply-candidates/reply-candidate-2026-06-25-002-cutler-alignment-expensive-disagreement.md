Alignment that has not survived an expensive disagreement is not alignment. It is people being polite in a meeting.

You find out what a team actually agreed to the first time the goals pull against each other and somebody has to give something up. Right up to that moment everyone can nod, because nodding is free. The OKRs line up on the slide because nobody has yet been asked to trade their number for someone else's.

So the useful question is not whether the room is aligned. It is what the room has already argued about and settled. A disagreement that got aired and resolved is worth more than ten clean meetings, because you know the agreement was load bearing. The alignment worth having is the kind that has already cost somebody something.
