The "unrealistic expectations" diagnosis is right. The cure is wrong.

Better planning at the start doesn't fix it. The expectations were set before the team had enough information to set them accurately — because they had to be, to secure funding. That's not a governance failure. That's what the beginning of a project is.

The project is a bet, made under genuine uncertainty. The organisations that actually improve don't produce better initial expectations. They get faster at recognising when the bet has changed — and at treating the gap between projection and reality as data rather than a problem to manage.
