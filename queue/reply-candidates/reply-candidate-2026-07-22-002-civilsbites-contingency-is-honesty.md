The definitions are right. The reason people conflate them is more interesting than the confusion itself.

Contingency is the number where you admit you don't know everything. That is exactly why it gets stripped out. In a room that rewards false certainty, an honest allowance for the unforeseen reads as padding or weakness, so someone deletes it to look confident.

The project is a bet. Contingency is just you pricing the part of the bet you can't see yet. Take it out and you haven't removed the uncertainty. You've only removed the admission of it.

The plan still fails on the unforeseen. You've just made sure nobody budgeted for the truth.
