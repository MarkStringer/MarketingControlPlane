The first month of stage three feeling worse is the most honest line in the post, and it's worth asking why.

I'd argue it isn't a trust problem. Reviewing every deliverable was your only source of bad news. It told you what was slipping before the client did. Stop reviewing and you haven't just let go of control, you've switched off the instrument you were reading.

That's why things ship that you would have flagged. Nothing got worse overnight. You simply can't see it any more.

So the move from stage two to three needs a replacement for the review, not just the nerve to drop it. Somewhere the team can say "this brief is shaky" while there's still time to change it, and be thanked for it.

Trust quickly is fine, provided the bad news has a route back to you.
