The case for proximity is right. A PM at arm's length from a delivery team is operating on second-hand information at best, and managed narrative at worst.

But proximity is necessary, not sufficient. A PM embedded in a team can still become part of the team's managed silence. You can sit in every standup, own the board, run the retrospectives, and still never learn the thing the team has decided is too uncomfortable to say out loud.

The real question isn't where the PM sits. It's whether the PM has created conditions where bad news travels without consequence. A PM who is physically inside the team but socially rewarded for producing green dashboards is just a more convincing source of the same problem.

That requires more than a change of desk.
