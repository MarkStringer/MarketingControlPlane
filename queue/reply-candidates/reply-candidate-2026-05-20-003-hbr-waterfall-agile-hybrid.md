The battle is worth ending, but not because a hybrid is better.

The choice of methodology happens at the moment you know least about the project. Waterfall asks you to specify what you're building before you understand it. Agile asks you to iterate toward a goal that turns out to keep moving. A hybrid does both, which has some value, but it doesn't resolve the underlying problem.

All projects are swamps. Swamps don't care which framework you packed. The real decision isn't Waterfall vs Agile vs hybrid. It's which set of assumptions you're prepared to be surprised by first — and how quickly you can update when reality arrives.
