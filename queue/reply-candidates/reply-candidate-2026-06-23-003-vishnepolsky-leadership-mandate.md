"You do not use leaders, you follow them" is a good line, and on most projects it describes something that simply isn't happening. The PM has been handed the accountability without the mandate that would make anyone follow.

Leadership isn't a property of the person, it's a relationship the organisation either permits or it doesn't. Put your best leader in the role, and if the sponsor won't back a decision and nobody acts on the call, that leader is just a very capable person being blamed for outcomes they were never allowed to shape. Point of view is worth 80 IQ points, but only when someone with authority is willing to act on it.

So I'd keep your diagnosis and move the fix one step up. The question isn't whether the PM can lead. It's whether the organisation has decided to be led. Most haven't, and no amount of personal leadership closes that gap on its own.
