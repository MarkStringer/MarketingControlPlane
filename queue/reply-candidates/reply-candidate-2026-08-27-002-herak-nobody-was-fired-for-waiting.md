The mechanics of this are right, and the three examples are better than most, because they are behaviours somebody could actually go and look at tomorrow.

Where I would push is on why it does not happen. Discipline is the usual explanation and I do not think it survives contact.

Start with the smaller problem. A behaviour is the cheapest thing in the world to produce on demand. Start measuring whether your strong people are having meaningful career conversations and I promise you will get career conversations. Thirty minutes, diarised, logged. You will not get meaning. The one virtue of attrition as a number is that it is almost impossible to fake, because it is people physically leaving the building.

But the bigger problem is not measurement at all. It is who is exposed.

A lagging indicator arrives with an alibi attached. By the time the margin has visibly gone, nothing is ambiguous, nobody has to back a hunch, and no individual made the call. The number made the call. You are not acting, you are responding, and responding is safe.

A leading indicator asks somebody to act while the evidence is still thin and while a perfectly reasonable colleague sitting opposite could look at the same signal and say wait.

Now price that. Act early and get it right, and nothing happens. That is the whole point of getting it right. The disaster you prevented leaves absolutely no trace, and there is no meeting where anyone thanks you for the quarter that was fine.

Act early and get it wrong, and you have spent real money on a problem that never turned up, and there is a record, and there are witnesses.

Invisible when you are right, expensive when you are wrong. Preferring the late number is not a lack of rigour. It is the correct read of the incentives, and the people doing it have read them properly.

So the discipline that is missing is not on the measurement side. Before anyone adds a single leading indicator, whoever is asking for them has to say out loud, in advance and in front of the same people, that acting on a weak signal and turning out to be wrong was still the right decision.

Say that and the indicators start working. Skip it and you have bought another dashboard, and it will be watched exactly as passively as the old ones.

Nobody has ever been fired for waiting for the number.
