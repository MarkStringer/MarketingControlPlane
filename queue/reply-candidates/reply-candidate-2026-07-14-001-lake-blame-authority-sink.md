The blame is real. But "you get blamed because you cared" is a story that makes it easier to keep standing there.

You get blamed because of where you sit, not how much you owned it. The PM is the one seat in the building handed responsibility for the whole thing without the authority to move most of it. Scope, deadline, the people who don't report to you. When the collective bet goes bad, and plenty of them do, the org needs one name to hang the loss on, and yours is the only one written against the entire project rather than a slice of it.

Caring harder doesn't change that. Standing in the fire longer just means you're the one still visible when the smoke clears.

The project is a bet the whole room placed. If you're going to carry the blame for it, the thing worth fighting for isn't credit or recognition. It's authority that matches the accountability. Refuse to own a number you're not allowed to change.

Bad news is data. So is which desk it always seems to land on.
