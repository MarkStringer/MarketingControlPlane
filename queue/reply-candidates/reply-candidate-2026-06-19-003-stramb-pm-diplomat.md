The diplomatic skills are real. The trap is that a skilled PM can use them to delay the hard conversation almost indefinitely. Managing stakeholders, building momentum, securing resources all become counterproductive if they are in service of an impossible brief.

Relationship-building only counts if it lands on honest expectations. Otherwise it is just a more comfortable way to arrive at the same bad outcome.
