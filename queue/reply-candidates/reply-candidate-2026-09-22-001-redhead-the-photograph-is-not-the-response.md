Every one of those things is true, and it's not just true of engagement surveys.

A project status report that goes red produces exactly the same failure. Somebody built beautiful reporting infrastructure, RAG ratings, trend lines, commentary fields, and none of it does anything, because nobody wrote down who acts on red before the first report went out.

Bad news is data. But data only changes anything if a specific person is on the hook to do something with it by a specific date. Without that, red is just another colour on a slide, and everyone in the room already knows it.

Your fix for surveys is the right fix for status reporting too. Decide who acts, on what, by when, before you ask the question. Otherwise you're not measuring the problem. You're photographing it.
