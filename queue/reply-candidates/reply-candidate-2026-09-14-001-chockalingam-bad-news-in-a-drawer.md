Profit is the story a project tells about itself. Cash is what happens when nobody's still writing it.

You're right that the P&L doesn't kill contractors. But look again at your own list: payments stuck in approval, variations left unassessed, retentions locked up for years. None of those are cash problems in the mechanical sense. They're bad news sitting in a drawer.

An unassessed variation isn't unassessed because nobody had time. It's unassessed because certifying it means someone has to say out loud that the number changed. A retention isn't held because the client forgot about it. It's held because somebody isn't yet willing to say the work can be trusted.

Bad news is data. A variation that hasn't been certified is data that's been refused. Cash flow doesn't fail first. Honesty does, and cash is just the thing that notices.
