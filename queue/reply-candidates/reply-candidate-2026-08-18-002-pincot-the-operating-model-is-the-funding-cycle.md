Agile transformed teams and left the operating model alone. That is exactly right, and I would go further on why.

The team is the last place in an organisation where you can change how the work happens without anybody giving anything up. Nobody loses a budget line when a team starts doing stand-ups. Nobody has to explain it to the board.

Go one level up and the operating model is not a way of working at all. It is the funding cycle. Money committed once a year against a business case, with names attached to the numbers.

Here's the thing. Running an organisation adaptively means letting someone hand money back in month four and say the bet did not pay off. Not reallocate it quietly. Say it out loud, in a room, to the people who approved it.

Calling that a leadership challenge makes it sound like it needs courage. Mostly it needs somebody who can report a loss and still have a job in the morning.

So to your question about what breaks first when things speed up. Not the decision making. The reporting.
