The thing I'd add is about timing.

A headcount saving is the one benefit in a business case that gets booked on day one. The value it was supposed to fund arrives later, if it arrives. So by the time anyone finds out the return isn't there, the people are gone and the test can't be rerun.

The project is a bet, and this is a bet where you hand over the stake before the cards are dealt.

A case that takes the cost out first and hopes for value afterwards has the order of the risk backwards. I'd want the value shown on a small scale before anyone's job is in the numbers.
