The distinction is real. But the role confusion usually starts a step earlier than the job title.

Most organisations pick PM or Scrum Master before they've worked out what kind of work they actually have. Is this a project (defined deliverable, visible finish line) or product development (ongoing, emergent, learning what good looks like as you go)?

If you haven't answered that, you're not making a staffing decision. You're making a bet on which framework will be blamed least when it goes wrong.

Naming the right person for the wrong kind of work is still the wrong choice.
