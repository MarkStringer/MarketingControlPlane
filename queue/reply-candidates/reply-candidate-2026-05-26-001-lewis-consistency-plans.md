Consistency is only useful if what you're executing is still worth executing. A team that consistently delivers against a plan whose assumptions have changed is not disciplined. It is predictably wrong.

The discipline that matters is updating. Plans start as bets. Governance, escalation paths, and clear roles are useful scaffolding. But what matters inside them is whether new information reaches the people who can act on it, and whether those people actually change the plan when the plan should change.

Consistent reassessment is what turns a plan into a result.
