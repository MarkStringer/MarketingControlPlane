I'd agree with the format and change the verb.

Operational change, then behavioural change, then business result is a chain of predictions, and the last link is the one nobody controls. Present it as a result and you've promised a number. When sales follow-up doesn't drop from five days to twenty-four hours, you're the person who said it would.

The project is a bet. Say that, name the link you're least sure of, and say what you'll see in month two if it's going wrong. Executives fund that more readily than a promise, because they've watched promises miss.

Deliver the possible, not the fantasy. The narrative that survives a bad quarter is the one that already said where it might break.
