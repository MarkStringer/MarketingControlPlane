The full-stack builder solves a real coordination problem. The overhead cost of splitting coding, design, and product thinking across separate specialists has always been high, and reducing it is genuinely valuable.

But there's a harder problem underneath: what do you build? That decision doesn't get easier when you can ship faster. It gets harder, because moving quickly in the wrong direction compounds.

Someone still has to be the person saying: this is the bet. This is what we're building and what we're not building. In the full-stack builder model, that judgment doesn't disappear — it gets absorbed by whoever has the most authority or the loudest voice.

Making the PM role invisible doesn't make the problem go away. It just makes it harder to find.
