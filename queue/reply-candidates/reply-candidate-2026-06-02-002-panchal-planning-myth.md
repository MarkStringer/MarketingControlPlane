If 90% of failures start in planning, the tempting conclusion is that better planning fixes it.

But the planning phase is where you commit to a course of action before you have most of the information you'll eventually need. Decision Gates help — they create moments of legitimate challenge. They also become theater when the organisation has already committed politically to the outcome and needs the project to proceed.

All projects are swamps. The front-end loading approach assumes the planning team can discover and price the uncertainty early, if they try hard enough. What they actually discover is the uncertainty they know about. The rest shows up later and gets called poor execution.

The information that would have changed the plan usually wasn't available when the plan was made. That's not a planning failure. That's what projects are.
