The risk calculation is the right diagnosis. I'd push on where that calculation actually happens.

"Start with one clear use case" gets presented as prudence. Often it's something else: a way of placing a bet small enough that nobody has to admit it's a bet at all. If the pilot fails, it was only a pilot. If it works, the real argument, about who owns the new shape of the organisation, starts anyway, and everyone quietly knew that argument was coming.

The unspoken question you named, "where do I fit if this works," isn't a fear to be managed down. It's the actual project. The tooling, the governance timeline, the literacy programme, all of it is administration sitting on top of a decision nobody has committed to making out loud yet.

Name the bet on day one and most of the resistance stops being resistance. It becomes people asking a fair question the organisation hasn't answered.
