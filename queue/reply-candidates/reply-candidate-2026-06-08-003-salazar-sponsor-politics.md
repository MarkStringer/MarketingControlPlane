The training gap is real. But I'm not sure it's why they disappear.

Most sponsors I've worked with understood their role well enough when things were going well. The disengagement started when the news got difficult.

And that is not a training problem. It is a political one.

The sponsor authorized the project. They spent political capital to make it happen. When bad news starts flowing upward, the natural instinct is not to be standing too close to it. You can prepare sponsors for their responsibilities in a classroom, but nobody teaches you what to do when the project you championed starts looking like a liability.

Until the organizational culture makes it safe to hear bad news without it becoming a career problem, the best-trained sponsor in the room will still find a reason to be in a different room.
