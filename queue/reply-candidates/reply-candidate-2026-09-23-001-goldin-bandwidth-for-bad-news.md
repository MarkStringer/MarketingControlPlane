Goldin's right that progress lives in the interfaces, not the individual talent. But latency and bandwidth aren't fixed properties of a team's plumbing. They're set by what's travelling through them.

Good news moves at full bandwidth, zero latency, every time. Everyone finds a channel for "we hit the milestone." The channel that goes quiet is the one that would carry "I don't think this is going to work."

That's not a communication problem. It's an incentive problem wearing a communication problem's clothes. You can redesign every interface on the team and the bad news will still take the long way round, because somewhere upstream somebody gets punished for being the one who said it first.

Point of view is worth 80 IQ points. But only if the system rewards whoever's holding it for speaking up, not for staying quiet until it's someone else's problem.
