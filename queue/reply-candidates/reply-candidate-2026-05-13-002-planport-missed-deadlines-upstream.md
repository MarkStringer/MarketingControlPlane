Ten causes, all upstream. That's a useful map.

But look at what's actually on it. Vague objectives. Unvalidated capacity assumptions. Late risk acknowledgment. Those aren't planning failures. They're what you'd expect from a plan written before the work had revealed what the work would actually take.

Most missed deadlines look like this: the plan was a bet, the bet came back with more accurate information than existed when you made it, and everyone kept treating the original timeline as if it were still true.

The fix isn't better upstream planning. You can't plan your way out of not yet knowing what you don't know. The fix is a shorter loop between new information and the plan.
