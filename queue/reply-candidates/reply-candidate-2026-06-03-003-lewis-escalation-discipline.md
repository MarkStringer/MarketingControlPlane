"Escalation discipline" is the most interesting phrase here. Most organisations do not lack governance rhythm — they have the weekly standup, the monthly steering, the quarterly review. What they lack is an environment where escalating bad news does not damage the person who escalates it.

Consistency creates results, yes. But the consistency that matters is not cadence — it is the consistent treatment of unwelcome information as data rather than failure. Without that, governance rhythm becomes a reliable schedule for hearing that everything is fine.

The cadence and the roles are easier to define than the psychological safety to tell the truth on time.
