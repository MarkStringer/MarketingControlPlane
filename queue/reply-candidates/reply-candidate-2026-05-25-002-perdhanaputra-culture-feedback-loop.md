'Culture' as an explanation for project failure is useful shorthand but tends to obscure the specific mechanism.

The mechanism is usually simpler: people who deliver accurate bad news to the right people at the right time get treated as problems. Not always fired - sometimes just ignored, overruled, removed from the next meeting. After a few of those experiences, they stop delivering bad news.

You can run Waterfall, Agile, Scrum, or any combination. That mechanism survives them all.

Bad news is data. The organisations that treat it seriously - not just in their stated values but in how they actually respond when they hear it - are the ones where frameworks of any kind start to work.
