Everyone agrees the human residue is emotional intelligence and diplomacy. I think that misses it.

The thing AI can't do isn't building trust. It's carrying bad news. AI will give you more bad news, faster, and with receipts. Risk analysis at machine speed is a firehose of things that are quietly going wrong.

Someone still has to stand in the room and say the interim solution is dead, then get the organisation to act on it before the money runs out. That isn't diplomacy. It's a particular attitude to bad news, which is that bad news is data.

The average PM won't be replaced by AI. They'll be replaced by the volume of honest information they were already busy avoiding.
