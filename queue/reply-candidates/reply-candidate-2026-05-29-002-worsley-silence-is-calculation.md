Silence usually means something specific. Not "I've lost interest." More like "I've done the math."

The stakeholder who stops attending has often spotted a problem and decided that being the person who raised it carries more professional risk than being the person who waited. They've watched what happens when bad news travels upward. They know.

Coming in with curiosity is the right instinct. But the most useful question isn't "how do we re-engage?" It's "what did they calculate?" What did they see that made going quiet the safer choice?

Bad news doesn't disappear because it's not spoken. It waits for a worse moment and arrives with receipts.
