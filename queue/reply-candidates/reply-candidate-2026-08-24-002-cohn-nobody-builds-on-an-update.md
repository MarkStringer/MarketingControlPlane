The distinction is a good one and most people don't make it. Productive and collaborative really are different things, and a smooth agenda hides the gap between them nicely.

But I'd look at what was on the table before I looked at how people were behaving around it.

Nobody builds on an update. An update is a report on something already settled, usually settled somewhere else, by someone who wasn't in the room. Questioning it is either pointless or it lands as an accusation, so people do the sensible thing and say nothing.

Ask questions before giving your opinion and you'll get better manners. You'll get collaboration when something in the meeting is genuinely still open, and the people in the room can tell that it is.

Put one undecided thing on the agenda and watch how quickly the curiosity turns up without needing to be rewarded.
