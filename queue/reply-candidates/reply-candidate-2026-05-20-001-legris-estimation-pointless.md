The discovery that no-estimates performs as well as story points doesn't show that estimation is pointless. It shows the value was never in the accuracy of the numbers.

What honest estimation forces you to do is have a conversation about what you know and what you don't. That conversation is the point. The number is the pretext for having it. Drop the conversation, and the uncertainty doesn't disappear — you just stop naming it together.

Your team succeeded with pair programming, TDD, and tight feedback loops — disciplines that surface problems early. Something was still doing the work of making uncertainty visible. The project is a bet either way. You just found a different way to call the odds.
