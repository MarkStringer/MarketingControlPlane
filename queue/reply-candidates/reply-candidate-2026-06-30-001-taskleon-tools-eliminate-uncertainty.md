Tools don't eliminate execution uncertainty. They make it visible. Those are very different products.

A project is a bet on something nobody has done in exactly this way before. The uncertainty isn't a coordination problem you can dashboard away. It's baked into the work. The best tool in the world gives you real-time visibility into a swamp. It does not drain the swamp.

What clear ownership and real-time visibility actually buy you is earlier bad news. That is worth a lot, because bad news is data and the sooner you get it the cheaper your options are. But sell it as eliminating uncertainty and you have trained everyone to trust a green dashboard right up until the thing fails.

Deliver the possible, not the fantasy. A tool that promises certainty is selling the fantasy.
