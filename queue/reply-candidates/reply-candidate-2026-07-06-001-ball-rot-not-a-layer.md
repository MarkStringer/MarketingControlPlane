You're right that the parable is too tidy. But moving the blame from the head to the gut is still hunting for the layer where it rots.

Culture doesn't die in mid-management. It dies wherever it stops being safe to say something is going wrong. Put a good manager in a system that punishes the messenger and they still can't get the truth up the chain. Put a mediocre one in a system that rewards early warnings and they will.

So the question isn't which layer rots first. It's whether bad news can travel from the floor to the top without someone paying for carrying it. Bad news is data. Most organisations treat it as an accusation, and that reflex runs top to bottom at the same time. That's the rot, and no single layer owns it.
