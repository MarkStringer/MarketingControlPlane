You're right, and I'd push it one step further.

The human part isn't warmth or trust in general. It's narrower and harder than that. It's whether the people around you will tell you the truth when the truth is bad.

A missed conversation slows the work, yes. But the conversation that never happens is usually the one where somebody knew a thing was going wrong and decided it wasn't safe to say. That silence is a human choice. It's also your worst technical problem, because bad news is data, and you were just denied the input.

So the technical side was never really about the tools. It's what you do with the information once people trust you enough to give it to you. The human job is earning the flow. The technical job is not wasting it.
