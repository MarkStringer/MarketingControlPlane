The observation is right. The frustrating part is why organisations build elaborate structures to systematically ignore it.

Proximity to the problem means proximity to bad news. And in most organisations, bad news is a career risk, not data. Information gets softened and filtered at every reporting layer until it becomes a status report, which is a kind of fiction.

By the time it reaches the people near the PowerPoint, the project has already been rendered safe for presentation. That's not a communication failure. It's the system working exactly as it was designed.

The fix isn't better communication channels. It's changing what happens to the person who delivers the bad news.
