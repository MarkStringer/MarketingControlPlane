The stakeholder who can sink your project is rarely the one on the RACI chart. It's the one nobody thought to invite, because inviting them would admit the project touches something you'd rather it didn't.

I spent three years on a project where the accessibility person and the security person turned up late, as if by magic, each holding a veto nobody had costed. They weren't on the edge by accident. Pulling them in early would have forced the plan to admit what it didn't yet know, and the plan was already promised.

That's the tell. The people left off the list are usually the ones standing on the part of the ground you didn't test. All projects are swamps, and the firmest looking patch is almost always the bit where you sent nobody to check.
