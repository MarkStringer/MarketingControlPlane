Delivery and budget aren't separable once things go wrong.

When the project hits trouble — and it will — the real question is: can we deliver this scope for this money, or do we change one of those things? If you've handed budget to finance, you're accountable for the outcome but ignorant of the constraint it sits inside. That's not liberation from distraction. That's negotiating without knowing what you can offer.

The project is a bet. The budget is what you're betting with. You can absolutely have a finance partner managing the spreadsheet. But you can't be accountable for delivery without understanding what the bet actually is.
