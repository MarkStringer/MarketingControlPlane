This is the test for anything we claim to care about on a project. Sustainability, quality, safety, all of it. The question is not whether it appears in the report. The question is whether it ever changed a decision when changing it cost money.

If the answer is no, it was never governance. It was decoration added at the end so the report would read well. You see the same move with risk logs that get written up after the risk has already landed.

The honest version is uncomfortable, because real governance means sometimes saying no to the cheaper option in the room, with the sponsor watching. Bad news is data, and a concern that survives only until it becomes inconvenient was never data to anyone. The project is a bet, and what you are actually betting on shows up in the decisions you make before anyone is reporting on them.
