Alignment decays, but not because stakeholders forget. It decays because the project changes underneath the agreement. What everyone signed up to in January is not what exists in June.

Which means repetition is the wrong tool. Keep re-selling the original story and you end up with stakeholders perfectly aligned to a project that no longer exists.

All projects are swamps. The people you're aligning have their own interests in the swamp, and the swamp moves. What needs refreshing isn't the message, it's the bet. That means going back with new data, including the unwelcome kind, and asking whether everyone still wants to place it.

Bad news is data. A cadence built on clarity, repetition and proof will quietly filter it out. The drift you're fighting is often the only honest signal in the room.
