'Ethical leadership builds trust' - agreed. But the constraint on project management is rarely a shortage of ethical project managers.

Most of the people I've seen misrepresent project status were uncomfortable about it. They'd learned, correctly, that accurate bad news travels upward and tends to come back down on the person who sent it.

Competence and integrity are necessary. They're not the leverage point. The leverage is in whether the people above the project manager treat accurate forecasting as useful information or as a failure of confidence.

Integrity is much easier to maintain when it doesn't cost you the project.
