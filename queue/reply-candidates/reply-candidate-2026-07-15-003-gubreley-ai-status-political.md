"AI cannot own decisions" is the right line. The trouble is the tasks you have handed it.

Status reports and RAID logs look like low-judgment admin. They are the most political documents on the project. The judgment in a status report is not the writing, it is deciding what counts as bad news and whether this is the week you say it. That call is most of the job, and it is exactly the part a model will smooth over, because a fluent green update reads better than an honest amber one.

Automating the artifact does not touch the incentive that corrupts it. A machine that drafts your status report faster just gets you to a confident wrong number sooner. Point of view is worth 80 IQ points, and the point of view no tool can supply is the one that decides the project is a bet worth telling the truth about. Start with the work, yes. Start with the work that no tool will do for you.
