Cutting once is the right lesson. I don't think kindness was ever the reason you cut three times instead.

Salami-slicing a layoff is what happens when you don't yet know the real number and aren't ready to say it out loud. Round one is what you could face that week. Round two is what you couldn't face yet. Round three is what the business needed all along, arrived at through exhaustion rather than analysis.

Bad news is data. The six months of uncertainty didn't happen because you told the team something hard. It happened because you told them a third of it at a time, small enough that nobody, including you, had to own the total on any single day.

The founders who cut once aren't braver than you. They just finished the sizing work before the first announcement instead of during the third one.
