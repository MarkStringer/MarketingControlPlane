Agree it isn't about control. I'd go one further on where the trust gap actually comes from.

A manager hovers when they don't believe the status they're being handed. And usually they don't believe it for a good reason. In most places the incentive is to keep the reds off the report, so the only reliable signal is the one you extract yourself by standing over someone's desk.

Micromanagement is what a project does when the truth only travels under pressure.

So the fix isn't really asking what they need to see to trust you. It's handing them the bad news before they come looking for it. Bad news is data, and a manager who gets it early stops needing to hover for it.

Trust follows the person who reports the problem they weren't asked about.
