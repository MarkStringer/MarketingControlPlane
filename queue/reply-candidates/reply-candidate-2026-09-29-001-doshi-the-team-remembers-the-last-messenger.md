You've got the direction of the fix right. A team that rehearses what the boss wants to hear is not lazy or timid. It is doing sensible risk management on the only information it has.

That information is what happened to the last person who brought something awkward into the room. Nobody works this out from a speech about openness. They work it out from one memorable Tuesday, and then the whole team carries the result forward.

So the leader's job is less "invite the hard topic" and more "be seen to spend the first thirty seconds after it arrives well." Thank the messenger, ask what they'd do about it, and change something visibly. Do that once and the pet-project theatre gets a lot thinner.

Bad news is data. A team that has learned to filter it out of the review will never get round to the customers, because the customers are not the ones who decide what happens to them.
