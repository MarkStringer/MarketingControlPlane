I'd push on "the person who goes first", because it sounds like a character trait and mostly isn't.

People wait for a signal because going first has a price. If the last person who said "this is going wrong" ended up owning it, everyone else has learned the lesson, and no plan changes that. The plan assumes someone will step forward. The room has already decided who won't.

So the useful question is what happened to the last person who went first. Bad news is data, and a team that punishes the messenger will get silence and call it a lack of initiative.

Write the first sentence into the plan. Name who says it, and what they get for saying it.
