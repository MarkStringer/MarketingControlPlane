The line I'd underline is the one about shutting down the same processes in the other factories. That's a serious reason to wait, and it has very little to do with looking as if you can't cope.

Escalating had a price you could see. Waiting looked free, because its price depended on the problem staying where it was.

So holding off was a bet. Of course it was tempting. Find the root cause first and you might only have to stop the right thing.

But from your account, what was on the table was every other site. The more it would cost to stop, the better the argument for giving the team another day, and the less that is one team's call to make.

Escalation is how you hand the bet to someone who is allowed to place it.
