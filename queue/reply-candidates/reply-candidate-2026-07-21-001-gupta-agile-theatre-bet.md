The cadence isn't the tell. You can ship every hour and still be running theatre if the release never changes your mind about anything.

Agile was never about small batches for their own sake. It was about shortening the loop between placing a bet and finding out you were wrong, so you can move the money before it's all gone.

The project is a bet. Doing the ceremonies faster doesn't make it a better bet. It just makes you wrong on a shorter cycle, unless someone is willing to read the result and change the plan.

That last part is the whole thing. And it's the part no framework can install for you.
