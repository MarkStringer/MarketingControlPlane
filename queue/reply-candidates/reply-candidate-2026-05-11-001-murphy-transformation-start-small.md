The four failure modes you list are real.

But there's a fifth that precedes all of them: the organisation commissioned a transformation it didn't actually want to make.

Every stakeholder nodded in the workshop. Every director signed the slide deck. And then every time the change touched an actual team, the answer was "not yet." Different meeting, same answer.

"Start small, scale what works" assumes the hardest part is adoption mechanics. Sometimes it is. But when the organisation's real goal is to demonstrate action rather than deliver change, starting small doesn't solve it. It just makes the failure smaller and harder to see.

The tell is what happens to the people who push hardest. If they're celebrated, you have a genuine transformation. If they're managed out or reassigned, you have a very expensive press release.
