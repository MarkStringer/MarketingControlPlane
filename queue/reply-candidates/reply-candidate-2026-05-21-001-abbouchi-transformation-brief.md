The distinction is real. A project manager who delivers on time and on budget while the organisation carries on exactly as before has, technically, succeeded. The programme is closed. The transformation hasn't happened.

But here's the thing: most organisations that commission transformation want the benefits without the disruption. Culture change without anyone being uncomfortable. Processes modernised without existing power structures shifting. The whole thing wrapped up on a schedule set before anyone knew what it involved.

That's not a hiring mistake. That's a brief that can't be delivered by anyone.
