NoEstimates and its critics are fighting over the wrong thing. The number was never the point.

The value of estimating is that it forces a team to look hard at the work before they commit to it. You walk the ground, you find the parts nobody understands yet, you argue about what is actually being asked. That looking is where the information lives. Skip it and you are flying blind.

The trouble starts the moment the number leaves the room and turns into a promise. Then everyone defends the figure instead of watching the work. So keep the estimating and throw away the idea that the estimate is a commitment. The project is a bet, and the estimate is your best read of the odds on the day you made it, nothing more.
