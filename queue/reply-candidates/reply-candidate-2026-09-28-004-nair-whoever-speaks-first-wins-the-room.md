The P&G system is a good fix, but I don't think efficiency is why it works. It works because of what doesn't happen to the junior view before anyone hears it.

When the senior person speaks first, everyone else in the room quietly edits their own opinion against it, whether they mean to or not. By the time it reaches the newest person in the room, what's left isn't their point of view, it's their point of view minus everything that might contradict what the room has already half decided. You don't get silence because juniors have nothing to say. You get silence because their actual answer was overwritten before they opened their mouth.

Reversing the order doesn't just build confidence. It's the only way to find out what the room actually thinks before the room decides what it thinks.

Point of view is worth 80 IQ points, but only the point of view nobody's edited yet. Ask your most junior person before your most senior one, every time, or you're not getting a second opinion. You're getting an echo with a two second delay.
