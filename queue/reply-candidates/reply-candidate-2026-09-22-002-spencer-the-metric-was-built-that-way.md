The 78% wasn't wrong. It was calculated exactly as specified, on scheduled time only, which is precisely why it was useless.

I see the project version of this constantly. A RAG status that's green because "green" is defined as "no open risk with an owner and a date," and every risk on that project is either unowned or undated, so there's nothing left to count. The metric isn't lying. It's measuring exactly what it was built to measure, and somebody built it that way, on purpose or by accident, and it barely matters which.

Your changeover number only existed because someone went and stood on the floor. That's the actual finding here, not the $2M. Dashboards report what they're told to report. Only a person standing where the work happens finds the gap between the number and the truth.

Deliver the possible, not the fantasy the dashboard is showing you.
