The plan was always the easy part. You could produce one in an afternoon.

What AI has done is remove the cover story. Strategy used to feel like work: whiteboard sessions, away days, decks with gradients. Now a prompt does it in thirty seconds, and everyone can see that it was never the bottleneck.

The bet isn't on the plan. It's on whether the people who matter will still be committed to the same thing six months from now, when the situation has changed and something else looks more attractive.

That's the part that's always been hard to execute. And AI isn't touching it.
