The three rows are the whole post and I would not argue with a word of the diagnosis. Three items, 38% of the money, the three most likely to move, and on the BOQ they look exactly like the row for door furniture.

But I would push on why it happens, because "they think they're managing costs" makes it sound like a habit of mind. I don't think it is. I think it is a perfectly sensible response to where the consequences are.

A BOQ is a contract document. Every row on it is a claim. Somebody gets paid on that row or refused on that row, this month, and there will be an argument about it with a person who has a name. Of course it is immaculate. Every line has an owner who will fight for it.

The Cost Plan is a forecast. Nobody gets paid on a forecast. Nobody gets blamed for one either, not until afterwards, and afterwards is a long way off when the valuation is due on Friday.

So the QS is not managing the list because they have confused it with the cost. They are managing the list because the list is where the trouble arrives. The forecast is where the trouble is. Those are different places, and the job pays you to stand in the first one.

Here's the other thing. The flatness you describe is not a fault in the BOQ. It is the point of it. A bill is flat so that every row can be priced and contested in the same way. Its job is to be fair, not to be true about risk. Expecting it to show you which three rows are the bet is like expecting the ruler to tell you the time.

Because that is what those three rows are. The other few hundred are the part of the project you know. Steel, MEP and finishes at 38% are the wager. The project is a bet, and the BOQ has helpfully formatted the bet to look like everything else.

One more thing, and it is a point in your favour rather than against. The 2.8 crores you avoided leaves no evidence. An overrun that did not happen is a number only the person who prevented it can see. Whereas every rate the QS negotiated down is right there on the page, with a before and an after. The list produces visible wins. The story produces invisible ones.

Which is one more reason the list keeps getting managed. It is not that people cannot see the future. It is that nobody has ever been thanked for it.
