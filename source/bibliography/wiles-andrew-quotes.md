# Andrew Wiles — Quotes

## Quote 1

> "Yes, some people are brighter than others but I really believe that most
> people can really get to quite a good level in mathematics if they're
> prepared to deal with these psychological issues of how to handle the
> situation of being stuck."

**Source:** Andrew Wiles (mathematician who proved Fermat's Last Theorem). Attribution only — exact original source not yet verified.

**Note:** Being stuck is a psychological problem before it's an intellectual one. Rhymes with "all projects are swamps" and "bad news is data" — the work is learning to stay in the difficulty, not being clever enough to avoid it.
