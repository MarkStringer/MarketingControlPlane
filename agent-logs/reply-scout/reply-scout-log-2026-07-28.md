# Replies drafted

- `queue/reply-candidates/reply-candidate-2026-07-28-001-ramdayal-coordination-is-the-wrong-defence.md` — argues Ramdayal defended the wrong thing. Coordination is the part that gets automated or absorbed, so it is a losing defence. The job nobody else will do is saying the true thing about the odds out loud when it is unwelcome. Grounded in the project is a bet and bad news is data.
- `queue/reply-candidates/reply-candidate-2026-07-28-002-willmott-value-axis-is-political.md` — argues the effort/value quadrant measures nothing about the scope item. Value belongs to the requester, so descoping is a political judgement dressed as a measurement, and effort estimates are least reliable in the corner everyone likes best. Real alignment is a named person conceding in front of witnesses.
- `queue/reply-candidates/reply-candidate-2026-07-28-003-kovalski-meetings-on-the-hook.md` — accepts Kovalski's conclusion, rejects his mechanism. Meetings do not beat documents at moving information; they beat documents at putting a person on the hook in front of witnesses. Proposes a different test: does something need to become true in public.
