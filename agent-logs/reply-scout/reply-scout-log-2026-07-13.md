Embedding a PM like that just moves the laundry closer to source. Embedding works only when the PM is loyal to what's true rather than to the report: sit inside the team, see the mess, carry it up without sanding off the corners. Bad news is data, and the PM's job is making sure it survives the trip up the hierarchy.
