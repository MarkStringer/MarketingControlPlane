# Replies drafted

- reply-candidate-2026-07-01-001-king-sunk-cost-incentive.md — Callum King (sunk cost). The sunk-cost story is the polite version; dead projects walk because killing one means standing up to say the bet lost, and nobody is rewarded for that. Reevaluation frameworks don't fix an incentive problem.
- reply-candidate-2026-07-01-002-parrish-decision-doors-unlabelled.md — Shane Parrish (cost-of-mistake/decision speed). The doors don't come labelled; you rarely know the cost of a mistake in advance, so the move is to buy information early rather than calibrate speed to a number you don't have.
- reply-candidate-2026-07-01-003-enejoh-preventable-failure-fantasy.md — Edward Enejoh (five preventable causes). Some bets lose no matter how clean the hygiene; treating all failure as preventable teaches people to hide trouble, which kills bad news exactly when you need it.
